Cheese Dip

Description

A warm beer cheese dip, made with dark lager and sharp Cheddar cheese, pairs well with Bavarian-style pretzels and bratwurst, and also works as a topping for sliders, or as a change to ordinary mac and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • All-purpse flour
  • Milk
  • German dark beer
  • Stone ground mustard
  • Worcestershine sauce
  • Grated sharp cheddar cheese
  • Crushed Aleppo chile peppers
  • Salt & Black paper

Steps

  1. Gather all ingridients
  2. Melt butter in a heavy saucpan over medium heat. Whisk in flour until smooth
  3. Continue whisking; pour in milk. Whisk in beer; cook until mixture thcikens, 3 to 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in mustard and Worcestershine sauce. Add chees; stir until cheese is melted. Stir in Aleppo pepper; Season with salt and black pepper
  5. Serve immediately
